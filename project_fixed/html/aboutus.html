History

Good Old’ Bakery began in a small suburban kitchen, filled every dawn with the rich aroma of butter and freshly baked bread. Founded on a deep love for traditional baking techniques, we preserve time-honored recipes that have stood the test of time for decades. To us, every slice of cake is more than just food; it is a story of dedication and quality that remains timeless.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To spread happiness through every bite of our freshly baked creations.

Our Mission

To deliver fresh, beautifully crafted baked goods that inspire moments of comfort and joy.

Meet The Owners

Behind the warm aroma of our every creation lies the dedication of two friends united by their love for the art of baking. Iyamoke, an artisan skilled in classic pastry techniques, brings a meticulous touch to every layer of cake.

Alongside Nanda, a visionary who explores modern flavors while staying true to traditional roots. They believe every loaf must have a unique soul and character that brings comfort to all who enjoy it.

Together, they built Good Old’ Bakery on the principle of honesty in selecting ingredients—from premium butter to natural leaven without preservatives. They believe this honesty is the key to creating authentic flavors that speak directly to the hearts of their customers.
